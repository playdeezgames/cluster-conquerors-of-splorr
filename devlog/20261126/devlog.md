Cluster Conquerors of SPLORR!! has been playable on GitHub Pages since November 10. It is finished, and today it gets a page here too.

It is a metaphor about paperwork, in the shape of a turn-based space strategy game. Pick a species of animal, survey a cluster of stars that sometimes lies to you, settle it with Moving Vans, run each colony on five chores, and build Golf Carts, Pickups and Monster Trucks until the cluster is yers. Every other empire hates you. They also hate each other.

- **Four cluster sizes**, from 20 stars to 72, with up to five rivals and five difficulty levels, from Quiet street to Cul-de-sac from hell.
- **The Homeowners' Association** meets every 25 turns. It hears complaints, levies fines, votes in bylaws and elects a President, who rules on every complaint.
- **Paperwork:** file pacts, trade agreements, tribute and threats at the forms desk, and send the Neighbourhood Watch to steal, sabotage and leave someone else's name on it.
- **Every rival has a temper and an aim**, and nobody tells you which. You find out from what they do.
- **Sound**, made in the Anvil. M turns it off.
- **Mouse, keyboard, gamepad and touch.** It saves itself in yer browser after every move.

Somewhere in the cluster is the Clubhouse. It has a porch light.

The latest version always lives at https://playdeezgames.github.io/cluster-conquerors-of-splorr/ and the itch build can lag behind it.
