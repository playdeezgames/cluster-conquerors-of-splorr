## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/cluster-conquerors-of-splorr/ (live since November 10, 2026; also add it as an external link on the page)

**Cluster Conquerors of SPLORR!!** is a metaphor about paperwork, in the shape of a turn-based space strategy game.

Pick a species of animal. Survey the stars, settle them, run each colony on five chores (Parking, Fencing, Home Improvement, Lawn Care and Book Club), and research hobbies until yer fleet of Golf Carts, Pickups and Monster Trucks can take the cluster. Every other empire hates you. They also hate each other.

- Surveys are filed, and sometimes misfiled. Some stars are not what the report said. Some are not stars.
- Ships burn fuel away from home. Run dry and wait for the Gas Can.
- New technology upgrades every ship at once. The fee is billed per ship.
- The Homeowners' Association hears complaints, levies fines, votes in a bylaw that binds everyone (quiet hours, no parking, no new houses...) and holds elections. Win one and you rule on every complaint: uphold it, dismiss it, or double it.
- Somewhere in the cluster is the Clubhouse. It has a porch light.

### How to play

Mouse, keyboard, gamepad and touch (landscape) all work. The game saves itself in yer browser after every move; "New cluster" under the screen starts again.

- **The map:** the arrows (or d-pad, or stick) move from star to star; a click or tap picks one. Enter/OK on one of yer colonies opens it; on a star with only yer ships, opens them.
- **A colony:** five sliders share what it makes (Parking builds ships, Fencing builds defence bases, Home Improvement builds factories, Lawn Care cleans up, Book Club researches). Up/Down pick, Left/Right move, Enter/OK locks; click a bar to set it.
- **Sending ships:** pick how many of each go, then the star; the line turns red when they would run out of fuel on the way. Ships fill up only at yer colonies, or from a Gas Can.
- **Research:** R (gamepad Y, the Tech button) shares Book Club's research among six hobbies.
- **Paperwork:** P (gamepad X, the Forms button) opens the forms desk: file a non-aggression pact, an alliance, a trade agreement, tribute in BJ or a tech, a research swap or a threat with a rival you have met, or tear it all up. Answers come back in the news. Rivals' forms land on yer doormat to sign or refuse.
- **The Neighbourhood Watch:** W (gamepad LB, the Watch button) sends a share of yer income to train watchers, then places them on a rival to watch, steal techs or wreck factories, bases or ships, and leave the other rival's name all over it. Caught is gone, and they will remember. A frame-up that fools nobody is read out at the next HOA meeting.
- **Battles:** pick a target, then fight a round, fight it out, or retreat.
- **Taking colonies:** Carpools land at an enemy colony once nothing armed is left to stop them.
- **T** (gamepad Start, the Turn button) ends the turn. **F** sends feedback.

This is slice 2 of 3, being built: one small cluster and two rivals, now with all ten species, diplomacy by filed forms, the Neighbourhood Watch with its frame-ups, bylaws, and a President who rules on every complaint. Random events, bigger clusters and more rivals are still to come.
